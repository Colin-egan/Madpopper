FAQ

Answers to common questions.

Is The Mad Popper popcorn nut free?

Yes! We are 100% nut free.

Is the corn used at The Mad Popper non-gmo?

Yes!

Are any of your popcorn flavors Gluten Free?

Yes! All of our standard flavors and fruity flavors are Gluten Free. We occasionally have Flavors of the Week that are made with ingredients that contain Gluten. We take precautions to insure that our machinery is clean and our Gluten Free flavors are not contaminated. Please feel free to ask before sampling or buying.

Do your flavors contain dairy products?

All of our savory flavors, as well as Caramel and Toffee, contain dairy. If you have a dairy allergy, please feel free to ask before you sample or buy.

Is The Mad Popper popcorn Kosher?

The Mad Popper is not certified Kosher, though several of our flavors contain 100% certified kosher products. Hekshering arrangements are available under Rabbi Daniel Greyber at Beth El Synagogue in Durham, NC.

Will you make special flavors upon request?

Yes! We are happy to fill special request orders. We do ask that you buy at minimum a Party Bag or All Occasion Tin of the flavor you are requesting.

Can I order popcorn on-line?

Yes! We have an online shop. Visit our online shop.

Do you ship your popcorn?

We ship our popcorn around the country Sept.-May. The summer months are too hot to guarantee fresh delivery.

Do you deliver?

Yes! We are happy to deliver our gourmet popcorn throughout the triangle area.

Do you have a list of all of your flavors?

Yes! See our flavors page.

Is The Mad Popper a franchise?

No. The Mad Popper is proud to be a locally owned and operated gourmet popcorn shop.

Where else can I purchase The Mad Popper popcorn?

The popcorn is available at Bulldega Urban Market and Beer Tooth Tap Room.
